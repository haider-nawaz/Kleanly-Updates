# Kleanly 2.2

- Move your license to a new Mac right from the activation window, even if the old Mac is gone.
- Reinstalling Kleanly on the same Mac now reactivates without using up your license.
- Opening Kleanly from Spotlight or Applications now shows Settings, so you can always find it when the notch control is hidden.
- New Share Usage Analytics setting and a link to the privacy policy.
- Kleanly now asks before opening at login.
- The notch control no longer checks the pointer when your Mac is idle, which saves energy.
- On displays without a notch, the control is a small tab that waits a moment before opening.
- Granting Accessibility permission no longer starts cleaning on its own.
- Added Contact Support to the activation window and About page.
